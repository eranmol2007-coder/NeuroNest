## 🎨 Design Details Matched

### Colors:
- **Background**: `#ffffff` (white)
- **Secondary Background**: `#f8f9fa` (light gray)
- **Text**: `#1a1a1a` (dark gray)
- **Secondary Text**: `#4a4a4a`, `#5a5a5a`
- **Button**: `#d4e7d4` (pale green)
- **Button Hover**: `#c5ddc5`
- **Dark Footer**: `#1a1a1a`

### Typography:
- **Font Family**: Inter (modern, clean)
- **Hero Title**: 2.5rem - 4rem (bold, white)
- **Section Titles**: 1.75rem - 3rem (semibold)
- **Body Text**: 1.0625rem (17px, readable)
- **Line Heights**: 1.6 - 1.75 (spacious, readable)

### Spacing:
- **Section Padding**: 80px top/bottom, 40px sides
- **Grid Gaps**: 40-60px
- **Text Margins**: 24px between paragraphs
- **Button Padding**: 16-18px vertical, 36-40px horizontal

### Layout:
- **Max Width**: 1200px (main content)
- **Narrow Width**: 900px (quotes, CTAs)
- **Grid Columns**: Auto-fit with minmax(320px, 1fr)
- **Split Layout**: 1fr : 2fr ratio
